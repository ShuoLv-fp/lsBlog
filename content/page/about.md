Field Notes is Shuo Lv's working notebook for ideas that benefit from more space than a project page or publication list.

- **Research Synthesis** gathers literature, methods, and technical notes.
- **Personal Experience** records applications, engineering practice, and lessons learned.
- **Life & Elsewhere** holds observations from outside the lab.

Tags describe what each note is about; categories describe why it exists. For a current biography, research interests, publications, and contact details, visit [Shuo Lv's academic homepage](https://ShuoLv-fp.github.io/).

Authors can preview the notebook's supported Markdown, mathematics, and reusable article blocks in the [Formatting Reference]({{< ref "/page/formatting-reference" >}}).
